**Source:** Market Researcher (2026-09-25).

**This file:** condensed English for the repo. Evidence ≠ truth. No invented ARR/users.

## Question

Among *existing* products and skills, which come closest to solving the Snowshoe problem — not “best product overall”?

## Scoring axes (equal weight 0–2, sum /10)

1. Personal *human* comprehension ledger  
2. Stale after pull/merge (git-delta anchored)  
3. Freshness signal (red / queue on delta)  
4. Transparent map + agency (human chooses what to learn)  
5. Verify / teach-back (agent does not paint green alone)

## Verdict

**Nobody scores 8+.** Shelf is partial pieces; exact wedge empty. Pedagogy (#1–2, #4) and structural freshness (#3, #5–6) live separately.
